\section{Class 5 model}
\label{sec:class5-model}

We apply the construction of Section~\ref{sec:setup} to the Class 5
chain.  After fixing the quantum model and spatial continuum limit,
we evaluate the finite-lattice time operator and its overlapping
products.  Their contribution to the continuum Lax equation is a total
spatial derivative.
We then compare the corrected Lax equation with the Hamiltonian
equations of motion and construct the time matrix independently from
the classical $r$-matrix.

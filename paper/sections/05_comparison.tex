\section{Comparison of Class 5 and Class 6}
\label{sec:comparison}

\subsection{Operator-product corrections in the coherent-state limit}
\label{sec:comparison-symbol-products}

Let $m\in\{5,6\}$ label the model and define
\begin{equation}
\Xi_m
:=\left[\bigl(L_m^{(1)}\bigr)^2\right]^\downarrow-U_m^2,
\qquad
U_m=\bigl(L_m^{(1)}\bigr)^\downarrow.
\label{eq:comparison-symbol-square-difference}
\end{equation}
All lower symbols are evaluated at the same spin
$\boldsymbol S(x,t)$.  Sections~\ref{sec:class5-time-lax} and
\ref{sec:class6-time-lax} give
\begin{equation}
\mathcal C_m
=2\ii\bigl(\partial_x\Xi_m+[\Xi_m,U_m]\bigr),
\label{eq:comparison-common-source}
\end{equation}
with the representatives
\begin{equation}
\Delta V_m
=2\ii\Xi_m-\frac{\ii}{4\lambda^2}\Id_{\mathcal V_a},
\qquad
V_m=V_m^\downarrow+\Delta V_m.
\label{eq:comparison-common-correction}
\end{equation}
The scalar term does not affect the curvature.

\subsection{Model-dependent terms}
\label{sec:comparison-model-dependence}

The different deformation scalings follow from the bond lower symbols.  For
Class 5, the deformation vanishes at coincident spins and its leading term is
proportional to $\kappa_5\epsilon$.  For Class 6, it contributes already at
order $\kappa_6$.  Matching these terms to the order-$\epsilon^2$ exchange
energy gives $\kappa_5=\epsilon\alpha_5$ and
$\kappa_6=\epsilon^2\alpha_6$.

Table~\ref{tab:class5-class6-comparison} compares the Hamiltonian densities
and spatial-operator coefficients.  Here $L_5^{(2)}:=0$ denotes the absent
second-order coefficient in Eq.~\eqref{eq:class5-L-expansion}, and $M_5$ and
$N_6$ are defined in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:class5-M-definition} and
\eqref{eq:class6-N-definition}.

The antisymmetric matrix $M_5$ couples the spin to its spatial derivative in
Class 5, whereas the symmetric matrix $N_6$ gives a local anisotropy term in
Class 6.  The corresponding second moments are
\begin{equation}
\Xi_5=\frac{\Id_{\mathcal V_a}}{4\lambda^2}-U_5^2,
\qquad
\Xi_6=\frac{\Id_{\mathcal V_a}}{4\lambda^2}
+2\bigl(L_6^{(2)}\bigr)^\downarrow-U_6^2.
\label{eq:comparison-two-symbol-squares}
\end{equation}
Thus $[\Xi_5,U_5]=0$, and the Class 5 source is a total spatial derivative.
For Class 6, Eq.~\eqref{eq:class6-L-first-square} expresses $\Xi_6$ through
$L_6^{(2)}$, and Eq.~\eqref{eq:class6-source-expanded} contains a nonzero
commutator.  The uniform configuration
Eq.~\eqref{eq:class6-uniform-source} isolates this term when all spatial
derivatives vanish.  In the general formula of
Section~\ref{sec:shared-site-common-form}, the explicit second-order
coefficient cancels after the intermediate products are combined, and
Eq.~\eqref{eq:common-Xi-from-U} reconstructs $\Xi$ from the leading spatial
matrix.

In the undeformed limit, write $\sigma_a^i$ for the Pauli matrices on the
auxiliary space $\mathcal V_a$.  Setting $\alpha_m=0$ gives
\begin{align}
\left.U_m\right|_{\alpha_m=0}
&=\frac{\Id_{\mathcal V_a}+S^i\sigma_a^i}{4\lambda},
\label{eq:comparison-isotropic-U}\\
\left.\Delta V_m\right|_{\alpha_m=0}
&=-\frac{\ii S^i\sigma_a^i}{4\lambda^2},
\qquad
\left.\mathcal C_m\right|_{\alpha_m=0}
=-\frac{\ii(\partial_x S^i)\sigma_a^i}{4\lambda^2}.
\label{eq:comparison-isotropic-correction}
\end{align}
Using $(S^i\sigma_a^i)^2=\Id_{\mathcal V_a}$ on the unit-spin constraint,
the corrected time matrix is
\begin{equation}
\left.V_m\right|_{\alpha_m=0}
=-\frac{(\boldsymbol S\times\partial_x\boldsymbol S)^i\sigma_a^i}
{2\lambda}
+\frac{\ii S^i\sigma_a^i}{4\lambda^2}.
\label{eq:comparison-isotropic-V}
\end{equation}
The direct symbol has twice the coefficient of the second term in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:comparison-isotropic-V}, so the shared-site correction is also
required in the spin-$\tfrac12$ XXX limit.

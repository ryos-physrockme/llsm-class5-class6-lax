\section{Quantum lattice setup and continuum limit}
\label{sec:setup}

This section formulates the continuum problem common to the two spin
chains.  We first fix the quantum lattice Lax equation and then define
the coherent-state lower symbols and long-wavelength scaling.  Keeping
the contributions from products on overlapping physical sites leads
to the condition that determines the correction of the continuum time
Lax matrix.

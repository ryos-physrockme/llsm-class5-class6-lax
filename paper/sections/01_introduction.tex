\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Landau--Lifshitz models describe long-wavelength spin dynamics and arise in
large-angular-momentum limits of string sigma models.  In the AdS/CFT
correspondence, their effective actions connect quantum spin chains to
classical strings \cite{Kruczenski2004,KruczenskiRyzhovTseytlin2004}.
Integrable deformations extend this comparison to anisotropic models and
backgrounds with reduced symmetry
\cite{KameyamaYoshida2014,DriezenLevkovichMaslyukMolines2026}.
Understanding how quantum integrability is realized in these continuum
field theories is an important part of this comparison.

Non-Hermitian chains raise further questions about exact solvability when
commuting transfer matrices have nontrivial Jordan blocks
\cite{deLeeuwPribytokRyan2019,CorcoranDeLeeuw2024}.  Recent studies of
Jordanian twists address generalized eigenvectors and spectral methods beyond
the usual highest-weight construction
\cite{DriezenLevkovichMaslyukMolines2026,BorsatoGarciaFernandez2025}.
Continuum limits provide a complementary setting for examining their
classical dynamics and Lax structures.

The Class 5 and Class 6 models of Ref.~\cite{deLeeuwPribytokRyan2019}
are non-Hermitian spin-$\tfrac12$ deformations of the Heisenberg XXX chain.
Their continuum Hamiltonians and higher conserved charges were derived in
Ref.~\cite{deLeeuwFontanellaNietoGarcia2026}.  That work also identified a
Jordanian Drinfeld twist for Class 5 and constructed a classical spatial Lax
matrix with a quadratic Poisson algebra, leaving a compatible time
component within that construction unresolved.  Related classical Lax representations are known for
the null-like warped $SL(2)$ Landau--Lifshitz model
\cite{KameyamaYoshida2014} and the rational eleven-vertex hierarchy
\cite{LevinOlshanetskyZotov2014,AtalikovZotov2021}; Class 6 is the
eleven-vertex model at the quantum level \cite{CorcoranDeLeeuw2024}.

Existing approaches to lattice and continuum integrability include
coherent-state limits of monodromies and Hamiltonians
\cite{AvanDoikouSfetsos2010,DoikouKaraiskos2011}, quantum auxiliary linear
problems \cite{DoikouFindlay2020}, and classical constructions based on
$R$-matrix identities \cite{DomanevskyZotov2025}.  Relating their local
time evolutions requires consistent treatment of the operator products in
the lattice zero-curvature equation.

Coherent-state lower symbols are expectation values over physical spin states.
Their nonmultiplicativity and exact finite-spin star products are well established \cite{Perelomov1986,KoczorZeierGlaser2019}.
Expectation values of operator products also enter earlier continuum
constructions of local Hamiltonians \cite{AvanDoikouSfetsos2010}.
At fixed local spin, products sharing a physical site retain the on-site
operator algebra as neighbouring spin labels become smooth.  For the local
Lax equation, the remaining question is which terms survive at the continuum
time-evolution order and whether their sum can be absorbed into a local time
matrix with ordinary matrix products.  This requires the lattice dynamical
identities together with the symbol calculus.

In this paper we establish this local correspondence for Class 5 and Class 6
using the algebraic commutator evolution.  Under stated assumptions on the
local interaction and spin-$\tfrac12$ long-wavelength expansion, the leading
shared-site terms cancel between the two lattice products.  Their spatial
Taylor terms and the next operator-product terms combine through the
Sutherland relation into a spatial derivative and a commutator of a local
matrix determined by the leading spatial Lax coefficient and its second
lower-symbol moment.  This form supplies the required correction to the time
matrix; explicit second-order spatial coefficients cancel after all terms are
combined.  The source is a total derivative in Class 5 and also contains a
commutator in Class 6.  The corrected zero-curvature equations are off-shell
equivalent to the independently derived Hamiltonian equations.  The Class 5 time matrix agrees up to a field-independent scalar with an
independent classical $r$-matrix construction.  Its traceless pair is locally
related, after complex field and auxiliary-space transformations, to the
null-like warped pair.  The Class 6 pair maps to the rational eleven-vertex pair.  The
correction is needed even in the undeformed XXX limit.  Numerical coefficient
searches \cite{KrippendorfLustSyvaeri2021} assisted the construction; the
final relations are established analytically.

We derive the continuum time Lax matrices for the Class 5 and Class 6
non-Hermitian deformations of the Heisenberg XXX chain from their quantum
lattice time operators.  The calculation uses coherent-state lower symbols
and a long-wavelength limit at fixed spin $\tfrac12$.  At the order of the
continuum time evolution, the lattice zero-curvature equation retains a
contribution from operator products sharing a physical site.  We evaluate
this contribution explicitly and absorb it into a local correction to the
direct time symbol.  The Class 5 contribution is a total spatial derivative;
Class 6 also contains a commutator involving the second coefficient in the
spatial-operator expansion.  For both models, identities with explicit
inverses establish the equivalence of the corrected zero-curvature equations
and the independently derived Hamiltonian equations of motion.  The Class 5
time matrix agrees with an independent classical $r$-matrix construction up
to a field-independent scalar matrix, and both models recover the isotropic
Landau--Lifshitz limit.  Numerical optimization of finite Lax ans\"atze
recovers the same coefficient relations, which are verified analytically.

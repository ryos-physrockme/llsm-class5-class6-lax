We derive continuum time Lax matrices for the Class 5 and Class 6
non-Hermitian deformations of the Heisenberg XXX chain directly from quantum
lattice time operators.  At fixed spin $\tfrac12$, products in the local
lattice zero-curvature equation share a physical site, so coherent-state lower
symbols do not factorize.  In the long-wavelength expansion, leading connected
terms cancel, while spatial Taylor and next operator-product terms survive at
the continuum time-evolution order.  Under the stated assumptions and
Sutherland relation, the surviving contribution is fixed by the lower-symbol
second moment of the leading spatial Lax coefficient and absorbed into a local
correction of the time matrix.  The correction is a total spatial derivative
in Class 5, whereas the Class 6 representation contains a nonzero commutator.
The resulting zero-curvature equations are off-shell equivalent to the
Hamiltonian equations.  The Class 5 pair is locally related to the null-like
warped $SL(2)$ Landau--Lifshitz pair, while the Class 6 pair maps to the
rational eleven-vertex pair.  In the undeformed XXX limit, omitting the
shared-site contribution gives an incorrect coefficient in the standard time
matrix.

We derive continuum time Lax matrices for the Class 5 and Class 6
non-Hermitian deformations of the Heisenberg XXX chain directly from their
quantum lattice time operators.  Starting from the local lattice
zero-curvature equation, we take a long-wavelength limit at fixed spin
$\tfrac12$.  Products in this equation share a physical lattice site, so the
coherent-state lower symbol of a product is not the product of the separate
symbols.  The leading connected terms cancel, while their spatial Taylor
coefficients and the next operator-product terms survive at the order of the
continuum time evolution.  Let $X$ denote the first coefficient of the
normalized spatial lattice Lax operator and set
$\Xi=(X^2)^\downarrow-(X^\downarrow)^2$.  For the spin-$\tfrac12$ scaling of
the two chains, the Sutherland relation gives the local source
$2\ii(\partial_x\Xi+[\Xi,U])$.  In Class 5 this source is a total spatial
derivative, whereas in Class 6 its model-specific representation contains a
nonzero commutator.  For both models, off-shell identities with explicit
inverses establish equivalence to the independently derived Hamiltonian
equations.  The traceless Class 5 pair is locally gauge-related, after a
complex field redefinition, to the null-like warped $SL(2)$
Landau--Lifshitz pair, and the Class 6 pair maps to the rational eleven-vertex
pair.  In the undeformed XXX limit, omitting the shared-site contribution
gives an incorrect coefficient in the standard time matrix.  Numerical
optimization of finite Lax ans\"atze recovers coefficients consistent with
the analytically verified pairs.

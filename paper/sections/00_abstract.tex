We derive continuum time Lax matrices for the Class 5 and Class 6
non-Hermitian deformations of the Heisenberg XXX chain directly from their
quantum lattice time operators.  Starting from the local lattice
zero-curvature equation, we take a long-wavelength limit at fixed spin
$\tfrac12$.  Products in this equation share a physical lattice site, so the
coherent-state lower symbol of a product is not the product of the separate
symbols.  The leading connected terms cancel, but their spatial Taylor
coefficients and the next operator-product terms survive at the order of the
continuum time evolution.  Let $X$ denote the first coefficient of the
normalized spatial lattice Lax operator and set
$\Xi=(X^2)^\downarrow-(X^\downarrow)^2$.  Under the spin-$\tfrac12$ scaling
realized by the two chains, the surviving source is controlled by $\Xi$ and,
when the corresponding Sutherland relation is imposed, takes the local form
$2\ii(\partial_x\Xi+[\Xi,U])$.  Class 5 reduces this contribution to a total
spatial derivative, whereas Class 6 has a nonzero commutator in its
model-specific representation.  For both models, off-shell identities with
explicit inverses establish equivalence to the independently derived
Hamiltonian equations.  The Class 5 pair agrees with an independent
classical $r$-matrix construction, while the Class 6 pair maps explicitly to
the known rational eleven-vertex Landau--Lifshitz pair.  The correction is
already required in the undeformed XXX limit.  Numerical optimization of
finite Lax ans\"atze recovers coefficients consistent with the analytically
verified pairs.

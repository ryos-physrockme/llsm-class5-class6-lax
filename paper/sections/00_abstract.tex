We study the quantum-to-classical Lax correspondence for the Class 5 and
Class 6 non-Hermitian deformations of the Heisenberg XXX chain.  Starting from
the finite-lattice Lax equation, we take a fixed-spin-$\tfrac12$
coherent-state long-wavelength limit.  The spatial component follows from the
leading lower symbol, but the time component is subtler because the two
products in the local lattice zero-curvature equation overlap on a physical
site and their lower symbols do not factorize.  We show that the leading
shared-site contributions cancel, while the next spatial Taylor and
operator-product terms combine, through the Sutherland relation, into a local
correction governed by the second moment of the leading spatial Lax
coefficient.  The resulting continuum zero-curvature equations are off-shell
equivalent to the independently derived Hamiltonian equations.  In Class 5
the source is a total spatial derivative, whereas in Class 6 it contains a
nonzero commutator.  The continuum Lax pairs are related to the null-like
warped $SL(2)$ and rational eleven-vertex Landau--Lifshitz representations,
and the undeformed XXX limit provides a nontrivial check.

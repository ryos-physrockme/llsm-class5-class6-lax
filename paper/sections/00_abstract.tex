We derive continuum time Lax matrices for the Class 5 and Class 6
non-Hermitian deformations of the Heisenberg XXX chain directly from their
quantum lattice time operators.  Starting from the local lattice
zero-curvature equation, we take a long-wavelength limit at fixed spin
$\tfrac12$.  Products in this equation share a physical lattice site, so the
coherent-state lower symbol of a product is not the product of the separate
symbols.  The leading connected terms cancel, while their spatial Taylor
coefficients and the next operator-product terms survive at the order of the
continuum time evolution.  Under the spin-$\tfrac12$ assumptions used here
and the corresponding Sutherland relation, the surviving contribution is
fixed by the lower-symbol second moment of the leading spatial Lax
coefficient and is absorbed into a local correction of the time matrix.  In
Class 5 this contribution is a total spatial derivative, whereas in Class 6
its model-specific representation contains a nonzero commutator.  For both
models, off-shell identities with explicit inverses establish equivalence to
the independently derived Hamiltonian equations.  After a complex field
redefinition, the Class 5 Hamiltonian density and equations of motion map
locally to those of the null-like warped $SL(2)$ Landau--Lifshitz model; the
corresponding traceless Lax pairs are related by an auxiliary-space gauge
transformation.  The Class 6 pair maps to the rational eleven-vertex pair.
In the undeformed XXX limit, omitting the shared-site contribution gives an
incorrect coefficient in the standard time matrix.

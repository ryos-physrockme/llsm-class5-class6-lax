\section{Summary and discussion}
\label{sec:summary-discussion}

We have derived the continuum time Lax matrices of the Class 5 and Class 6
Landau--Lifshitz models from their quantum lattice time operators at fixed
spin $\tfrac12$.  In the local lattice zero-curvature equation, the two
operator products share a physical site.  Their coincident
order-$\epsilon^2$ contributions cancel, while the first spatial Taylor terms
and the coincident order-$\epsilon^3$ terms remain at the continuum
time-evolution order.

For the operator expansion of Section~\ref{sec:shared-site-common-form}, the
surviving contribution is expressed through
$\Xi=(X^2)^\downarrow-(X^\downarrow)^2$.  With the corresponding Sutherland
relation,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal C=2\ii\bigl(\partial_x\Xi+[\Xi,U]\bigr),
\end{equation}
and the source is absorbed into a local correction of the time matrix.  The
Class 5 and Class 6 lattice calculations reproduce this expression and give
zero-curvature equations that are off-shell equivalent to the independently
derived Hamiltonian equations.

In Class 5, $[\Xi_5,U_5]=0$ and the source is a total spatial derivative.  In
Class 6, the representation of $\Xi_6$ through $L_6^{(2)}$ gives a nonzero
commutator, which remains on a spatially uniform spin configuration.  The
explicit second-order spatial coefficient is needed in the intermediate
operator products but cancels from the general expression for $\mathcal C$.

The resulting classical pairs agree with independent constructions.  The
Class 5 time matrix matches the classical $r$-matrix/monodromy result up to a
field-independent scalar matrix, and its traceless pair is locally related to
the null-like warped $SL(2)$ Landau--Lifshitz pair after the field and gauge
transformations of Section~\ref{sec:class5-known-null-pair}.  The
transformation is not generically periodic.  The Class 6 spatial matrix,
time matrix and spin equation map to the rational eleven-vertex
Landau--Lifshitz pair through the dictionary in
Section~\ref{sec:class6-eleven-vertex-comparison}.  In the undeformed XXX
limit, the direct time symbol has twice the correct coefficient of one term;
the shared-site correction restores the standard isotropic time matrix.

The numerical searches in Appendix~\ref{sec:ml-search} recover coefficients
consistent with the analytic Class 5 and Class 6 pairs at the tested parameter
values.  The final Lax relations are established by the operator calculation
and the off-shell curvature identities.

The derivation is local on the complex unit-spin constraint surface and uses
the fixed spin-$\tfrac12$ scaling.  Periodicity enters the Hamiltonian
variation and the Class 5 monodromy comparison.  The comparison with quantum
dynamics concerns instantaneous derivatives on product coherent states;
finite-time control would require estimates of the correlations generated
between sites and of the accumulated continuum error.

For higher lattice Hamiltonians, the time operators act on more physical
sites and the overlap terms can involve higher local operator moments.
Comparing those flows with the boost-generated charges of
Ref.~\cite{deLeeuwFontanellaNietoGarcia2026} and the eleven-vertex hierarchy
of Ref.~\cite{LevinOlshanetskyZotov2014} would test the extension of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:common-source-general}.  A separate extension to higher local
spin would clarify the relation between the fixed-spin long-wavelength limit
used here and a large-spin classical limit.
